# Data

`script/helper/simulate-data.R` generates 26 trials of one trait on 30 genotypes, in the shape of the Musa data in Brown et al. (2023). Trials differ in the metric used to score the trait (a disease index where lower is better, days to symptoms and youngest leaf with symptoms where higher is better), in whether block data or only trial means are reported, in the genotypes tested (6 to 15 of 30, with the reference in most trials), in the number of crop cycles (one or two) and in the climate of the evaluation period. Every third trial also reports a second metric with gaps. Three trials fail the inclusion criteria on purpose, one without coordinates, one without an end date and one whose genotypes carry local names that match no other trial.

Seven genotypes are dry-sensitive. Their resistance drops when the longest dry spell in the evaluation period exceeds 13 days. Rain on wet days is scaled so that rainfall totals are similar across sites, and temperature has no effect. The truth per cycle is in `sim$cycles` (`true_mlds`, `dry`) and the latent resistance and the sensitive genotypes in `sim$truth`.

To use your own data, place it in this folder and document it here (variables, units, metric direction, source and licence), with one table of observations (trial, cycle, block, genotype, metrics), one of trial metadata (coordinates) and one of evaluation periods per trial and cycle (start and end dates). Then replace the simulation call in `script/01-analysis.R` with calls that read them, and replace the simulated climate with daily rainfall and temperature from a documented source, for example AgERA5 through ag5Tools or CHIRPS and NASA POWER through the chirps and climatrends packages.
