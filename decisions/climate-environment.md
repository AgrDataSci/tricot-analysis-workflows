# Climate and environmental data

Status: methodological rationale from Kauê de Sousa (Bioversity International), 2026-10-31. This is not independent validation of cited studies or software.

- Choose climate windows from documented days to phenological events (flowering, booting, harvest) or from growing degree days estimated with climatrends and suitable temperature data.
- Record crop and variety phenology, its source, thermal thresholds, planting dates, climate source, spatial and temporal resolution, and uncertainty.
- Explore both environmental similarity (analogues) and predictive modelling. Define the target population of environments, assess domain shift and uncertainty, and avoid automatic recommendation claims.
