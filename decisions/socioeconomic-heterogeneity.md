# Socioeconomic heterogeneity

Status: methodological rationale from Kauê de Sousa (Bioversity International), 2026-10-31. This is not independent validation of cited studies or software.

- There is no universal preferred method. Choose among subgroup analyses, trees, clustering, covariate models and other defensible methods according to the study goal.
- Goals include explaining heterogeneity, defining breeding target users and making differentiated recommendations. Do not conflate them.
- Segmentation should produce interpretable, practically usable groups, with transparent trade-offs and representation checks.

- **Few groups, few participants.** With two or three groups (regions, projects) and fewer than about 30 valid answers per group, check the likelihood-ratio test with a permutation of group labels. Worth plots often look different between groups when the test is not significant. Write "the groups look different but there are not yet enough answers to tell", never "the groups prefer different items", and do not put such a claim on material for participants.
- **Few covariates.** Do not cluster participants when only a handful of covariates were recorded (for example age, gender and region). Compare the predefined groups instead.
